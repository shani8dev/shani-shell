# What's new on this desktop

A short tour of what this system does — the full list is in FEATURES.md on
the machine (and in the shani-shell source). Everything here is a click or
a glance away, and when you are not using it, it costs almost nothing.

## Sign in without typing

- **Face sign-in** — look at an infrared camera, like Windows Hello. Works
  on the sign-in screen and the lock screen.
- **Fingerprint** — touch the reader to unlock.
- **Security keys** (YubiKey and other FIDO keys) — touch it to unlock or
  sign in. Your password always works too.
- **An on-screen keyboard at sign-in, setup and lock**, opening by itself
  on touchscreens, with a **Caps Lock warning** and **Show password**.

## A desktop that stays out of your way

- **Bar, dock and Island** — workspaces, the focused window, now playing,
  status at a glance. The middle of the bar (the Island) shows what is
  happening — a recording, a call, a timer — and a click acts on it.
- **One launcher for everything** — apps, open windows, files, actions
  like "dark style" or "lock", a calculator, emoji, clipboard history and
  web search. It forgives typos: "fierfox" finds Firefox.
- **Lens** — select part of the screen to read its text, scan a QR code,
  copy, search or translate — or ask Chronoa, which sees it.
- **Chronoa** — the companion in the middle of the bar. Ask it from the
  Island, the launcher, a notification or a selection; it answers
  approvals in place.

## Power and protection

- **Battery saver** at 20 %, a **charge limit** to keep the battery
  healthy, and a power mode that can follow the charger (Settings > Power).
- **Sleep, then hibernate** on laptops so a flat battery never loses your
  work.
- **Earlier versions of your files** from daily snapshots, and **backups
  to another drive**.
- **ShaniOS updates** shown in Quick Settings, with a reboot nudge when
  one is ready — and this note, once per version, after an update.

## Make it yours, without hunting

- **Settings you pick, not type** — every choice is a list to tap, never
  a value you have to know.
- **Light and dark** that follow the time of day, fading gently between
  the two (or instantly, with reduce motion on).
- **Wallpapers** — your own, a slideshow, one per screen, or found online
  with one search. Accent colour can come from the wallpaper.
- **First setup in six short steps**, in six languages, with a screen
  keyboard and accessibility options from the first screen.
- **Keyboard and game controller** drive every surface: arrows in reading
  order, Enter to press, Escape to go back.
